## Exact prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: a standalone mobile app icon master for the Chinese photo album brand 相角.
The attached reference image is a BRAND IDENTITY REFERENCE. Use ONLY its left emblem as the visual basis. Remove all Chinese lettering, all wordmark layout, and the surrounding landscape composition.

Create ONE finished SQUARE 1:1 app-icon image, ideally 1024 x 1024 pixels.
Preserve the reference emblem's identity: a large softly rounded triangular photo-mount shape at lower left and a smaller paper/photo shape at upper right, separated by a clean diagonal negative-space opening running from upper left toward lower right. The emblem suggests a photograph gently tucked into a paper photo corner. Keep the silhouette recognizable and the two shapes simple; do not introduce a complete camera or a surrounding photo frame.
Refine the geometry into clean, flat, solid-color artwork with consistent softened outer corners and a crisp diagonal gap. Keep that gap wide enough to remain recognizable at small icon sizes. The shapes should feel warm, quiet and thoughtfully crafted, with stable optical balance.

Composition: just this single emblem, optically centered in the square. The emblem fills about 64–68% of the canvas width, leaving generous equal visual breathing room around all edges. One flat background that reaches every edge of the canvas. Keep the master image square WITHOUT baked-in rounding of the outer canvas corners; mobile operating systems can apply their own icon mask.
Palette: solid warm ivory background #F2EFE8; solid deep warm charcoal emblem #302C26. Clear separation, no gradients, no textures, no shadows, no highlights, no 3D effects.
Deliver only the finished icon artwork. No text or letters of any language, no miniature app name, no phone mockup, no device frame, no external drop shadow, no border, no watermark, no multiple variants, no presentation sheet.
