## Exact generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Create ONE refined, finished logo for a Chinese instant-photo album web app named exactly “相角”.
This is a new logo generation, not an edit of any conversation image.
Brand context: digitizing and cherishing physical instant photographs, keeping their fronts and backs, dates and handwritten stories. The interface is warm ivory paper, delicate charcoal typography, restrained and tactile.

Logo idea: a distinctive, compact emblem built from FOUR traditional triangular photo-mount corners, carefully framing the negative-space silhouette of an instant photograph. Make it read as photo corners gently holding a cherished print, rather than a generic camera, crop tool or QR scanner. One or two tiny negative-space folded-paper cuts may give the mount corners a subtle signature. Deliberate geometry, balanced optical spacing, a wider bottom margin evocative of instant film, extremely clear silhouette, legible when reduced to a small app header icon. Elegant, warm, quiet, collectible.

Composition: a single horizontal logo lockup, emblem on the left, the two Chinese characters 相角 on the right. The emblem is about the same optical height as the wordmark. Generous but controlled space between them. Center the lockup on a wide canvas with clean, comfortable margins.
Wordmark text VERBATIM: 相角
Render both Chinese characters correctly and beautifully, with a bespoke contemporary Songti-inspired serif wordmark: fine-to-medium contrast, confident structural strokes, subtly softened terminals and meticulously balanced character spacing. Readable, sophisticated, human. Not calligraphy, not cartoon typography.
Color: one solid deep warm charcoal ink (#2E2C27) for the complete emblem and wordmark. The negative spaces stay transparent.
Medium: flat, vector-like, crisp 2D logo artwork. TRUE transparent background / alpha channel. No filled background, no checkerboard drawn into the image.
Deliver only the final isolated logo. No mockup, no photograph, no paper sheet, no texture, no shadows, no gradients, no embossing, no English subtitle, no tagline, no extra lettering, no palette, no design board, no alternate versions, no watermark.
